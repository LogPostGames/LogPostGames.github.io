Typography Guidelines

Verbal Tone & Voice

Our verbal tone shapes how we communicate across social channels, press releases, and game copy. We put clarity before cleverness and prioritize warmth, curiosity, and authenticity.

Imaginative & Cozy

We invite players into warm, intriguing worlds, but we are never overly convoluted or cryptic.

Heartfelt & Playful

We treat our players and games with affection, humor, and respect without leaning into cynicism or sarcasm.

Welcoming & Clear

We speak plainly and passionately. We avoid marketing jargon and prioritize clear communication over hype.

Boilerplate

Log Post Games is an independent game studio dedicated to crafting imaginative adventures with heart, humor, and technical ambition. We believe in exploring the most interesting version of every idea and invite players to discover worlds that feel both surprising and deeply personal.
