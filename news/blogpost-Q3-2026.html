Kickstarter Pre-launch - Q3 2026 Quarterly Review

Hello, hello! This is going to be a shorter one because I’m hard at work but wanted to make sure I still give proper updates.

Tricks of War Kickstarter

Work is coming along great on Tricks of War! I’ve recently worked with an incredible digital linocut artist to make the game’s box art, and I’m very in progress on a lot of carving for the game.

I’m working on nailing down all the Kickstarter rewards and prices, but with this being a smaller indie print run the game might cost a little more than expected unless I can get the cost down further than I already have. We’re looking at the Kickstarter pre-launch page going up by the beginning of November and we’re on track for a February launch!

I am trying to find any content creators or reviewers that are available to check out the game. Indie marketing budget is closer to 0 than I would like, but if you know anyone who wants to make content with a free copy of the game just reach out.

PAX Unplugged

In fun playtesting news, I’m bringing Tricks of War to PAX Unplugged in December! I’ve got a nice prototype with not final art and I’ll be bringing it to PAX with Unpub! Tricks of War was at Origins with Unpub, so this will be fun to run back in my hometown.

The game is coming along nicely and stay tuned for November for some big announcements.

- Jess Case, Founder of Log Post Games
